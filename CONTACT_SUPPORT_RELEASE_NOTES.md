PAL Contact Support update — 2026-11-02

Adds a small gray Contact Support mailto link next to Terms and Privacy in the shared application footer and legal-page footers. It opens the visitor’s email client addressed to admin@palbiz.app (the contact already published in PAL Terms and Privacy). No passwords, tokens, or company data are sent by the application.

Limitations: This is an email link, not an in-app ticketing system. Email delivery and inbox monitoring have not been verified. It does not change OAuth API error handling, intuit_tid capture, or persistent troubleshooting logs. Do not answer Yes to those Intuit questions based on this release.
