# Before deploying the PAL policy pages

These are legal drafts, not lawyer-approved final policies. Review and replace the draft notice and effective dates in `public/terms.html` and `public/privacy.html`.

Confirm and document: (1) verified PAL contact email/address; (2) actual hosting, storage, authentication, AI providers and subprocessors; (3) actual encryption/access safeguards; (4) retention/deletion process; (5) Intuit disconnect procedure; (6) any customer agreements and governing law; (7) applicable Intuit developer policies. Do not submit draft pages as finalized compliance documents.

Changes made: two public Express routes `/terms`, `/privacy`; small legal footer in shared HTML template; footer CSS. Existing API, bots, and auth routes were not edited.
