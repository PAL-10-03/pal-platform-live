Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PAL Business Solutions, LLC (“PAL”) handles information in connection with its business automation platform and website.

1. Information processed

Depending on customer use, PAL may process account and contact details, user identifiers, uploaded receipts and documents, invoices, vendor and contract records, accounting data, employee-related operational records, messages, system logs, and information provided through connected services such as QuickBooks Online.

2. Purposes

PAL uses information to authenticate users, provide tenant-specific workspaces, process uploaded documents, perform customer-authorized workflows, produce reports, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and support the service.

3. QuickBooks information

With authorization from an eligible QuickBooks administrator, PAL may access permitted company accounting data to provide the features the customer enables. PAL should access only the connected company's authorized records. PAL does not sell QuickBooks data or use it for unrelated advertising. Access can be revoked through Intuit's connected-app controls; additional disconnection options may be offered within PAL.

4. AI and service providers

Customer-provided information may be processed by configured AI services and infrastructure providers when needed to deliver enabled features. These providers may include authentication, hosting, storage, and AI processing services. PAL currently uses third-party infrastructure and authentication services and may use configured AI processing providers to provide customer-authorized features. The applicable providers and processing arrangements depend on the enabled services.

5. Access controls and security

PAL is designed to restrict customer workspaces and data to authorized users. No online system is risk-free. PAL uses the access-control and security measures actually implemented in its production systems; security certifications or encryption guarantees should not be assumed unless independently verified.

6. Retention and deletion

Records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, satisfy applicable legal or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, or maintain security. For questions about retention or requests for deletion, contact admin@palbiz.app. Requests are reviewed subject to applicable legal and accounting obligations. Disconnecting QuickBooks stops future authorized access but may not automatically erase previously processed records.

7. Sharing

PAL may share data with service providers needed to operate the platform, with third-party integrations the customer authorizes, or when required by law. PAL does not sell customer financial records to advertisers.

8. Customer responsibilities and rights

Business customers determine which users may access their information and are responsible for their lawful collection and use of personal data. Requests to access, correct, or delete information can be directed through PAL's published contact channels, subject to applicable law and retention requirements.

9. Changes and contact

PAL may update this Policy as its services or legal obligations change. For privacy inquiries, data access or deletion requests, contact admin@palbiz.app.
